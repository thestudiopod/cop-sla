01 Purpose

How Software & Service actually runs.

This is the service attachment for ConocoPhillips pods. It is not a rewrite of the rental or purchase agreement. It is who does what, how fast we respond, and what changes if you take the Enterprise tier.

Two tiers. Core is the Software, Service, and Technical Support ConocoPhillips already signed. Enterprise is the additional support tier requested for a multi-site, multi-year rollout: SSO and security, HRIS and badging export, a dedicated CSM, extended hours across your time zones, and warranty for the life of the term.

What Core already covers · as signed

Software and Service. Cover any issues with the software and maintain updates for the duration of the rental/term. Service the equipment if it breaks down; schedule to come on site and replace it to get the Pod up and running again.

Technical Support. Provide support when the Pod or software isn’t functioning properly, beyond what is fixed by a normal restart. The client team is first line of defense for: Pod needing to be turned on (power outage), reconnected to internet, or restarted (most issues are quickly fixed with a simple restart). Anything beyond that, or if repeated restarts don’t correct an issue, Studio Pod is available during normal business hours.

Normal business hours: Monday-Friday, 9:00am-5:00pm CT. Phone (346) 375-5454. Email hello@thestudiopod.com. Same-business-day reply on general email. Year 1 of service includes remote support, software updates, lighting calibration, and a guaranteed-replacement SLA.

04 Roles

Who does what

ConocoPhillips · first line

Power, network, restart, then call

  • Turn the Pod on after a power outage.
  • Reconnect it to the internet if the network dropped.
  • Restart the Pod. Most issues clear with a simple restart.
  • If it still fails, or if it fails again after restart, call the number on the Pod sign: (346) 375-5454.

A password prompt, a locked session, or a pod that will not capture is a Sev 1. Call from the Pod so we can remote in. Do not wait to email later.

Studio Pod · second line

Anything that survives a restart

  • Software defects.
  • Remote diagnosis while someone is at the Pod.
  • On-site replace when hardware is down, scheduled to get the Pod running again.
  • Updates, lighting calibration, and (Enterprise) morning health checks across sites.

We are available during the support hours of the tier you are on. Core is 9-5 CT. Enterprise is 6am-8pm CT.
